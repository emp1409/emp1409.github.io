Puzzle Baron

I often use this website to help practice for this type of cipher called an Aristocrat Cipher1.

  1. An aristocrat cipher replaces the plaintext (original text) with ciphertext (encrypted text) through the use of a substitution alphabet shifted by a keyword. However, Puzzle Baron uses what's termed as a "Random" or "Plain" Aristocrat, where the substitution alphabet is arranged in a random order instead. You can find more information here.
